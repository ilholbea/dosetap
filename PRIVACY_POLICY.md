# Privacy Policy — DoseTap

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Summary

DoseTap collects no personal data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## What data does DoseTap collect?

**Nothing.** DoseTap does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal information, health information, or usage data.

## Does DoseTap use the internet?

No. DoseTap has no `INTERNET` permission in its manifest. It is physically incapable of making network requests.

## Where is my data stored?

All data (medication names, dose timestamps, settings) is stored exclusively in a local Room database and DataStore on your device. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Export feature.

## What does the Export feature do?

The Export feature creates a JSON file containing your medication configuration and dose history. This file is only shared when you explicitly tap "Export" and choose where to send it (email, Drive, etc.). DoseTap does not receive or store this file.

## Does DoseTap use analytics or crash reporting?

No. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, Firebase, or any third-party SDKs in DoseTap.

## Does DoseTap access the camera, microphone, or contacts?

No. DoseTap only requests these permissions: notifications, exact alarm scheduling, boot completion (to restore alarms after reboot), vibration, and wake lock (to show the alarm screen).

## Children

DoseTap is intended for adults managing their own medication. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If DoseTap ever introduces any data collection (it is not planned), this policy will be updated and users will be notified via an app update.
